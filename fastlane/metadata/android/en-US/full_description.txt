<p><b>Type Malayalam the way you text: in Manglish.</b> Type <i>ente keralam</i> and get എന്റെ കേരളം.</p>

<p>Ezhuthola (എഴുത്തോല, the palm-leaf manuscript) is a free, open-source Malayalam keyboard. It asks for <b>no permissions at all</b>, not even internet, so nothing you type ever leaves your phone. No ads, no accounts, no tracking.</p>

<p><b>Malayalam (Manglish)</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Type in English letters and get Malayalam: njan → ഞാൻ, adipoli → അടിപൊളി</li>
<li>Its own transliteration engine, written from scratch</li>
<li>Word suggestions ranked by how often Malayalam words are really used</li>
<li>Word forms with endings: keralathil → കേരളത്തിൽ</li>
<li>Learns your words: the spellings you pick come first next time</li>
</ul>

<p><b>English</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Word suggestions and completion</li>
<li>Autocorrect, with backspace to undo it</li>
<li>Knows chat words like bro, tbh and ngl, and leaves your Manglish alone</li>
<li>A Missing words list in settings shows words it doesn't know yet, so you can add them</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Everything else</b></p>
<ul>
<li>One tap on the yellow ola key switches between Malayalam and English</li>
<li>Emoji search in English, Manglish and Malayalam: love, chiri, sneham, ചിരി</li>
<li>Clipboard history: tap to paste, hold to pin. Passwords and OTPs are never saved</li>
<li>Themes: Numio, Black &amp; White, Light, Blue Night, or your own photo</li>
<li>Number hints on the top row, ₹ on both symbol pages</li>
</ul>
